The cold playtest (Thu ~1700, while dinner cooks)

This is the highest-yield playtest available all week, because it is the only one run by someone who does not know the intended path. Pip's own Thursday-morning playtest proves the build works; this one probes whether a stranger can get in.

  • Hand over the shipped artifact -- the zip/exe that build_release.py emitted. Not the editor, not a dev build. What they play must be what Friday's players get.
  • Do not coach. Not one hint. The cold open and progressive-disclosure onboarding are live design questions (#814); a coached playtest destroys exactly the signal worth having.
  • One full run to game-over is the target -- roughly the length of cooking. It also exercises the game-over -> leaderboard transition, the path that segfaulted in v0.11.0.
  • Watch, do not discuss. Note where they hesitate, what they click that does nothing, and anything they say out loud. Design conversation can wait for dinner; it contaminates the run.
  • Capture: the in-game bug reporter for anything broken; your own notes for hesitations. The F7 capture rail is dev-build only (#991), so it will not be in this artifact.
  • Triage the same night, briefly: soft-lock or crash is league-critical -> Friday ~1500 re-cut. Confusion or roughness is NOT league-critical -> it is Saturday hotpatch or next month's content, and it should not touch Friday's build.

The contingency that actually matters

If Thursday morning finds a defect, the re-cut window is Friday ~1500 (playbook line 101) -- which is 1h45 before [Gate 5: SEED BLESSING] at 1645. That is thin. So:
  • Anything found Thursday morning gets triaged that morning: league-critical or not. No overnight ambiguity.
  • League-critical -> fix Thursday morning, re-cut, re-run the whole [Gate 4] sequence. Not a patch on top.
  • Not league-critical -> it goes to the Saturday hotpatch window, not into Friday's build. Friday's build is finished Thursday.
The rule that protects the league: after the freeze, any gameplay-shaped change means re-cut, not patch. Two builds reporting the same epoch while playing differently is a lie the ladder guard cannot catch -- both honestly report L3.
