## v0.13 -- the launch build (what you download)

The v0.13 epoch is the friends-and-family launch. Highlights:

- **A real hiring pipeline** -- advertise roles, interview and onboard
  researchers; candidates apply; onboarding steps (laptop, mentoring) surface as
  costed actions rather than happening off-screen.
- **Onboarding that meets you at the door** -- a narrative cold-open and a
  first-launch welcome overlay, so a new player gets direction instead of a wall
  of buttons.
- **An office you can see** -- a visual office floor in the WATCH phase; the
  walkers mirror your staff count. New backdrops, icons, and art throughout.
- **The league is live** -- weekly-seeded leaderboards: a fresh seed every
  Friday, a new balance epoch each month, so scores stay comparable within a
  season.
- **A much smaller download** -- the build dropped from ~418 MB to ~59 MB by
  purging unreferenced hi-res art.
- **Legibility + stability** -- action costs shown on the buttons, unaffordable
  options greyed rather than dead-ended, the ledger now reads in days and dates
  (not turns), plus a raft of soft-lock and crash fixes.

## v0.13.1 -- polish & honesty pass

A small patch on the same leaderboard epoch as v0.13.0 (non-forking -- runs and
scores stay comparable):

- The main-menu line is now the game's true spine:
  **"You can't win. You can only buy time."**
- Removed a placeholder "Office Maintenance" action that charged money and did
  nothing.
- Fixed a data-validation check that was failing continuous integration.
- Refreshed the developer-facing READMEs to the current pure-GDScript
  architecture (the old Python bridge is long gone).

No gameplay or scoring changes in v0.13.1.
